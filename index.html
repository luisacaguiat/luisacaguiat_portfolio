ABOUT ME

Hello! I'm Ma. Luisa Andria Ramirez Caguiat, born on January 23, 2004, and currently living in Bambang, Bocaue, Bulacan. I am a 4th-year Computer Engineering student at Bulacan State University - Meneses Campus, specializing in programming, microcontroller systems, logic design, and computer networking.

I am passionate about turning ideas into real projects, such as developing a Robocar, an Automatic Trash Bin, and an Attendance Monitoring System. These projects reflect my interest in applying engineering principles to solve everyday challenges.

Aside from my technical expertise, I value being detail-oriented, adaptive, and communicative, which helps me excel both in independent work and collaborative settings. I actively take part in organizations, serving as Secretary and Multimedia Assistant at ICpEP.SE - BulSU Meneses, where I enhance my leadership and creative skills.

Looking forward, I aim to hone my skills further, embrace new challenges, and contribute to meaningful innovations in technology that improve lives and communities.
